Wet loontransparantie: Quick scan

What applies to you & what to do

Answer a few questions about what your company already has. At the end you get your own path in three buckets — recruitment, the right to information, gender pay gap reporting. Every no comes back as a requirement with the article and how to build it; every yes as the standard it has to meet and how to check it. You only ever see your own branch.

This tool asks about systems and documents, never about people or their pay. Nothing you click leaves your browser — no personal data is processed, so there is nothing here the GDPR restricts. The one exception is optional and at the end: if you type in your email address and press send, that address and your results go to FairWave so Anca can send you the results.

Built by Anca Dranca, founder of FairWave. Pay transparency, job architecture and pay equity for employers in the Netherlands and the EU. anca@fairwave.nl · LinkedIn
